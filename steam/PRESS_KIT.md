## In one line

A fallen Demon Lord rebuilds a floating pixel-art citadel with self-directed monsters and holds it against 100 waves of human and mecha invaders.

## Description

IsoChronicle: Idle Realms is an offline-first isometric idle strategy game. You wake as a weakened Demon Lord in the ruins of your citadel, with only a Support Slime for company. The Slime summons an Ancient Treant, the Treant rebuilds the castle and summons thirteen Generals, and every General raises an establishment with its own minions. There's no build button: the realm runs itself while you steer it through upgrades, skills, runes, the market and your own sky lasers in battle.

Invaders pour from four corner rifts in shifting formations across 100 waves and four realms (Demon Citadel, Magma Caldera, Frost Spire, Astral Sanctum). Generals with a grudge brawl each other to grow stronger, minions slip into the human realm on expeditions and come back with loot (and enemies), and once you conquer wave 100 you can regress and start again with permanent boosts.

## Features

- A living 20×20 floating island in hand-painted pixel art: day/night, seasons, storms, snow, heatwaves and animated water life.
- 13 establishments, 13 Generals and their minions, each with jobs, expeditions and skills.
- 100 invasion waves with random formations, bosses that drop runes, and invaders that answer your expeditions with vengeance.
- Double-click sky lasers that burn and slow invaders.
- General brawls, mirror invaders and a split-screen live view of the fights.
- Regression (prestige), a ranked skill tree, runes and realm choice.
- Fully offline: no account, no ads, no microtransactions. Playable in English and Tagalog.
