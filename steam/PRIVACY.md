# Privacy Policy: IsoChronicle: Idle Realms

_Last updated: 19 October 2026_

IsoChronicle: Idle Realms ("the game") is made by SimplyEd. This policy covers the Steam (desktop) version and the browser version of the game.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The game has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no telemetry. It doesn't send your data to us or to anyone else.

## What stays on your device

- **Your save** (realm progress and settings) is stored only on your computer: in `%APPDATA%/IsoChronicle/saves/` (Windows) or `~/.config/IsoChronicle/saves/` (Linux) for the Steam version, or in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) for the browser version.
- **Steam Cloud**: if you have Steam Cloud turned on, Steam copies that save folder to your Steam account so it follows you between computers. Valve handles that under the [Steam Privacy Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/).
- **Steam Achievements**: when you unlock an achievement, the game tells your local Steam client, which records it on your Steam profile under Valve's policy.

## Optional music player

The in-game music player can play YouTube links that **you** paste in. Those videos load from YouTube, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies to them. The game only stores the links in your save.

## Children

The game doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on the game's Steam page and in this file. Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/dipaaapi/isochronicles-idle-realms> or use the contact listed on the Steam developer page.
